# Manifest trademarks

The name "Manifest" (as the name of this product) and the Manifest logos are trademarks of the project owner. The code is open source; the name and logos are not. This policy keeps people from confusing a fork or a hosted copy with the original.

## What you can do without asking

- **Say what your product is built on.** "Based on Manifest", "built with Manifest" or "a fork of Manifest" in your description, About screen or documentation. The attribution line required by the [NOTICE](NOTICE) file is always allowed.
- **Say what works with Manifest.** "Compatible with Manifest", "works with Manifest" or "a Manifest plugin" for software that connects to Manifest or its API, MCP server or agent plugin.
- **Redistribute it unmodified.** You may pass on unmodified copies of Manifest under its original name and logos.
- **Write and talk about it.** Articles, papers, reviews, tutorials, talks and news coverage can use the name and show the logos to refer to the project.

## What needs a different name

- **Forks and hosted versions.** If you change Manifest, or run it as a service for others, give your product a different name and logo. You can still say it is based on Manifest.
- **No implied endorsement.** Do not suggest that the Manifest project or its owner sponsors, endorses or supports your product, service or organization unless you have written permission.
- **No confusing names.** Do not use names, logos, domain names or social handles that could be mistaken for Manifest (for example "Manifest Pro" or "Manifest Cloud" for your own fork).
