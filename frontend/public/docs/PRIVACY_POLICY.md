# Manifest Privacy Policy

Last updated: **November 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Manifest collects, uses, shares, and stores personal information when you use our website, applications, APIs, account tools, AI-assisted features, messaging, collaboration, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

This document is intended to be product-ready and engineering-aligned, but it is not a substitute for legal advice.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect when you:

- visit Manifest websites or landing pages,
- create an account or use a guest session,
- use AI-assisted workspace features,
- submit forms, surveys, or support requests,
- interact with collaboration, messaging, or discovery features,
- or use our APIs and product integrations.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information.

### A. Information you provide directly

- account information such as email and profile details,
- startup notes, ideas, prompts, uploaded content, and generated workflow edits,
- collaboration and messaging content,
- contact form submissions,
- survey answers and product feedback,
- account-security inputs such as MFA enrollment data and recovery workflows,
- and support or operational communications.

### B. Product and technical usage information

Depending on your settings and our configuration, we may collect:

- session and authentication events,
- browser/device metadata,
- feature interaction data,
- page and route usage,
- performance telemetry such as web-vitals and long-task metrics,
- and product analytics events.

Optional analytics are controlled through our privacy preferences and consent mechanisms where required.

### C. Security and operational information

We may collect information needed to secure the Service, such as:

- IP address or an irreversible salted hash of IP data,
- session identifiers,
- user-agent data,
- audit and security logs,
- login, password reset, MFA, and session activity,
- and abuse-prevention or rate-limit signals.

### D. Guest session data

If you use Manifest without a full account, we may create a temporary guest identity and workspace. Guest data may be deleted, expired, or anonymized according to our retention rules.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide, maintain, and improve the Service,
- authenticate users and secure accounts,
- generate and store requested AI-assisted outputs,
- power collaboration, messaging, publishing, and discovery workflows,
- respond to support and contact requests,
- measure product quality, onboarding, and reliability,
- enforce policies, prevent abuse, and comply with law,
- and communicate with you about your account, requested support, or product updates when you have allowed that communication.

## 4. AI and Automated Processing

Manifest uses AI and automation to help users create documents, structure ideas, surface suggestions, and operate startup workflows.

This means:

- your prompts and workspace inputs may be processed to generate outputs,
- automated systems may classify or summarize product activity,
- and generated outputs may be stored so they remain available in your workspace.

You are responsible for reviewing generated outputs before relying on them. Do not submit sensitive information unless you are comfortable with it being processed for the requested workflow and protected under your own legal/compliance requirements.

## 5. Tracking, Cookies, and Consent

Manifest uses a combination of strictly necessary storage and optional analytics-related storage.

### Strictly necessary

These are used for:

- authentication and session continuity,
- security and abuse prevention,
- saving core privacy choices,
- and keeping the product operational.

### Optional analytics

Where enabled, optional analytics may be used to understand:

- onboarding and activation,
- feature usage,
- survey engagement,
- performance and reliability,
- and other product-improvement signals.

Optional analytics are disabled by default in our current privacy architecture until a user provides the relevant choice where required.

When Ora Agent Front reporting is configured, Manifest's Cloudflare edge sends Ora limited request events to show which automated agents and pages reach the site. Events can include the requested host and path, HTTP method and status, allowlisted attribution parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `ref`, and `source`), a referring site's origin, user-agent, country code, and response duration. If an Ora salt is configured, the edge creates a daily pseudonymous visitor value from the connecting IP address, user-agent, date, and salt. The raw IP address, cookies, and other query parameters are not sent to Ora. Ora receives these reports as a service provider for agent-traffic analytics.

### Privacy controls

Users can manage privacy choices through:

- the consent banner,
- account privacy settings,
- and related in-product privacy controls.

These preferences may include:

- product analytics on/off,
- communications preference,
- and a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" style opt-out.

For more implementation detail, see our internal engineering documentation for tracking and consent.

## 6. Legal Bases and Regional Rights

Depending on your location, we may process information under different legal bases, including:

- performance of a contract,
- legitimate interests,
- consent,
- and compliance with legal obligations.

### GDPR / UK GDPR

If you are in the EEA, UK, or similar jurisdictions, you may have rights to:

- access your personal data,
- request correction,
- request deletion,
- object to certain processing,
- restrict processing,
- request portability,
- and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

### California / CPRA

If you are a California resident, you may have rights to:

- know what personal information we collect and use,
- request deletion subject to legal exceptions,
- correct certain information,
- opt out of certain sale/sharing activity,
- and exercise these rights without unlawful discrimination.

Manifest currently provides product-level privacy controls and account-level data-management tools aligned with these principles, but specific legal workflows may still require operational support handling.

## 7. How We Share Information

We may share information:

- with infrastructure, hosting, analytics, communications, authentication, and AI service providers acting on our behalf,
- with other users when you intentionally publish, share, collaborate, or message through the platform,
- with professional advisers, auditors, or acquirers in connection with legitimate business operations,
- and when required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and platform integrity.

We do not say in this policy that we "sell" personal information in the ordinary commercial sense. However, some jurisdictions define "share" broadly, and our privacy controls are designed to support those opt-out expectations where relevant.

## 8. Founder, Investor, and Marketplace Context

If you use discovery, publishing, collaboration, investor-facing, or marketplace-like features:

- information you intentionally publish or share may be visible to other users,
- messages and collaboration activity may be stored so the feature works,
- and startup-related profile/activity information may be surfaced as part of the product experience.

You are responsible for deciding what to disclose publicly or to other users.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to:

- provide the Service,
- maintain user workspaces and requested outputs,
- protect the platform,
- comply with legal obligations,
- resolve disputes,
- and enforce agreements.

Examples of retention-related behavior in the product may include:

- guest-workspace expiration windows,
- password-reset expiry windows,
- audit and security logging retention,
- and retention of consent/audit records.

When data is no longer needed, we may delete, aggregate, or anonymize it.

## 10. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure. You should use a strong password, enable MFA when available, and avoid sharing credentials.

## 11. Children

Manifest is not intended for children under 13, and you should not use the Service if doing so would violate age-related laws in your jurisdiction.

## 12. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your own. Where required, we may rely on contractual, technical, and organizational measures intended to support lawful cross-border processing.

## 13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If material changes are made, we may update the date above and provide notice through the Service or by other reasonable means.

## 14. Contact and Requests

If you have a privacy request or question, use the product's support/contact channels or other official contact methods provided by Manifest. If you are making a legal rights request, include enough information for us to verify and respond appropriately.
