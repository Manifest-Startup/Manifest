Turn your startup idea into a plan

Manifest helps founders turn a raw startup idea into a structured workspace and a set of practical drafts. Describe the idea, then develop its business model canvas, pitch deck, product requirements document and provisional patent draft. Review and edit generated content before you rely on it. Manifest is currently a demo and its materials are not legal, financial or investment advice.
