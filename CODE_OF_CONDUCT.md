# Code of Conduct

Manifest follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and welcome people of every background and experience level. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language and publishing others' private information are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to `hello@manifeststartup.com`. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participants who break this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
