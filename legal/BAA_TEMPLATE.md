# Business Associate Agreement (TEMPLATE)

> Template — customer-specific BAAs are negotiated and stored separately.
> Effective only when signed by both parties. Last revision: 2026-04-19.

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is entered into between
**[Covered Entity]** ("Covered Entity") and **Manifest, Inc.**
("Business Associate"), and forms part of the Master Subscription
Agreement ("MSA") between the parties.

## 1. Definitions

Capitalised terms not otherwise defined have the meanings set out in
45 CFR Parts 160 and 164 (the "HIPAA Rules").

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures

Business Associate may use or disclose Protected Health Information
("PHI") only to perform the services described in the MSA, for proper
management and administration of Business Associate, or as Required by
Law.

## 3. Safeguards

Business Associate shall implement administrative, physical, and
technical safeguards meeting the HIPAA Security Rule (45 CFR
§§164.308, 164.310, 164.312), including encryption at rest and in
transit, role-based access control, MFA for privileged accounts, and a
hash-chained audit log.

## 4. Reporting

Business Associate shall report to Covered Entity:
- Any Use or Disclosure of PHI not permitted by this BAA — within 5
  business days of discovery.
- Any Security Incident involving PHI — within 24 hours of confirmation.
- Any Breach of Unsecured PHI — within 5 business days of discovery,
  with sufficient detail for Covered Entity to comply with its
  obligations under 45 CFR §§164.404-410.

## 5. Subcontractors

Business Associate shall ensure that any subcontractor that creates,
receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on behalf of Business Associate
agrees in writing to the same restrictions and conditions that apply
to Business Associate.

## 6. Access, amendment, accounting

Business Associate shall, within 15 business days of a request, make
PHI available to Covered Entity in a Designated Record Set, accept
amendments, and provide an accounting of disclosures per 45 CFR
§164.528.

## 7. Termination

Upon termination, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI
and retain no copies, except where return or destruction is infeasible,
in which case the protections of this BAA shall survive.

## 8. Survival

The obligations of Business Associate under §§3, 4, 6, 7 survive
termination of the MSA.
